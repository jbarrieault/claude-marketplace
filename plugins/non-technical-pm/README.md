# non-technical-pm

Summarizes or explains recent work, or a recent answer, briefly and at a high
level for a non-technical Product Manager.

This is the best way to have your agent explain something to you, once your ego can accept it.

Invoke with `/non-technical-pm:explain`, optionally naming what to cover
(`/non-technical-pm:explain what you did`, `/non-technical-pm:explain the caching fix`). Claude
also loads it on its own when you ask for a PM-friendly summary.

## Skills

- `explain` — the summary prompt.
